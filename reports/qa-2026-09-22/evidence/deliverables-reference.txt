04 — TECHNICAL WORKFLOW — AS NARRATED BY DR. SWETA ADATIA
The Journey Inside the Platform
   "When I designed DecodeMyBrain, I wanted every family to feel that science was working quietly in the background — not that they were filling
   forms. Here is what actually happens the moment someone trusts us with their brain."
   — Dr. Sweta Adatia, MBBS MD DNB(Neurology) FACP MBA(Cambridge), Founder

 1 You arrive. We do not ask for much — just your name, email, and date of birth. REGISTRATION
      That date of birth is everything. Our system instantly looks at it and routes you to the correct program — Quest for a child between 12 and 14, Evolve for a teenager
      from 15 to 18, Summit for an adult. No dropdown. No confusion. The brain science knows your stage of life already. Under the hood, the Laravel backend writes your
      record to the users table in MySQL, hashes your details, and stamps a dob field that drives every decision from here.
 2 You pay once. We unlock a lifetime of insight. STRIPE CASHIER
      Stripe handles your card. When payment clears, a webhook fires instantly to our server. Our StripeWebhookController receives it, verifies the signature, and calls
       EntitlementService to write your package slug — decodemybrain-deep-dive or equivalent — to the users table. The RequirePaidPackage middleware now
      opens every assessment route for you. This takes less than two seconds after you click Pay.
 3 An email arrives with your key. LARAVEL MAIL
      Immediately after entitlement is confirmed, our mail system sends your credentials — username, auto-generated password, and a direct link to
       app.decodemybrain.com . The email goes from info@limitlessbrain.com. There is no waiting room. You open the email, click the link, and you are inside.


 4 The  assessment begins. 100+ questions that reveal your whole brain. QUESTIONSCONTROLLER
   Your questionnaire is not a personality quiz. It is a neuroscience-backed whole-brain map. The QuestionsController serves questions one at a time from the
       questions table, storing each answer in question_answers linked to your session. Beyond the core 100+ questions, there are dimensional questions (left-brain /
      right-brain balance), an introvert–extrovert dimension, a skill assessment, and interactive game-style tasks — all gated by the AuthQuestionAnswered middleware
      so you cannot skip ahead. Your progress is saved after every single answer, so a refresh never loses work.
 5 Your brain profile is computed. SCORING ENGINE
      When you submit the last answer, our scoring engine reads all your responses from question_answers , dimensional_question_answers ,
       introvert_extrovert_question_answers , and skill_test_question_answers . It calculates your scores across five brain dimensions — Analytical, Creative,
      Emotional, Practical, and Intuitive — and writes them to brain_scores and user_dimensional_score . This is the moment your unique brain blueprint is born. It
      usually takes under three seconds.
 6 Your PDF Report is generated. A personalised blueprint, not a generic printout. BRAINRESULTSCONTROLLER
      The BrainResultsController queries your scores and passes them to a Blade PDF template. The template combines your dimension scores, career suggestions,
      learning strategies, and parenting or self-development insights based on your program. A percentage score per brain quadrant is displayed as a visual chart. The PDF
      is saved to public/reports_pdfs/ and linked to your dashboard. You can download it at any time. The admin can also access it from the Super Admin panel.
 7 You book a coach. We close the loop. CONSULTATION BOOKING
      After your report, your dashboard shows a booking option. Clicking it takes you to a two-step consultation booking flow — pick a time slot, confirm. The booking is
      saved to the database and an email notification goes to the DecodeMyBrain team. This is optional — the report alone is a complete, stand-alone deliverable. The
      coaching session deepens it.

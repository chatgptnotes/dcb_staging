01 — Mission & Locked Decisions
One Native Laravel App. No WordPress. No WooCommerce.
The logged-in member platform — assessments, scoring, PDF reports, Stripe checkout — is already built and live at app.decodemybrain.com. Users land on limitlessbrainlab.org and are redirected to the app. The rebuild adds the public frontend and a simplified pay-first purchase flow around it. Nothing in the existing member engine changes. The program (Quest / Evolve / Summit) is not selected by the user — it is auto-mapped after registration based on date of birth.
Pay-First Funnel
No free plan. Register and pay in one step. Credentials emailed after payment. Assessment unlocks on first login.
Plan Mapped After Registration
Program is never selected before or during registration. DOB entered at registration automatically maps to Quest, Evolve, or Summit after payment.
WordPress Cord Cut
WooCommerce replaced by Stripe Cashier. WP user bridge retired. Native MySQL becomes the single source of truth.
02 — Individual User Purchase Flow
7 Steps from Discovery to Brain Report

Registration and payment happen in one step — no separate sign-up. Stripe Cashier handles the one-time payment. Credentials are auto-emailed post-payment. No free tier exists. Grey steps (6 + 7) are existing Laravel code — Assessment and Book Coaching are NOT to be built by us. They are retained as-is from the live member platform at app.decodemybrain.com. Users enter via limitlessbrainlab.org and are redirected there.

04 — Technical Workflow — As Narrated by Dr. Sweta Adatia
The Journey Inside the Platform
"When I designed DecodeMyBrain, I wanted every family to feel that science was working quietly in the background — not that they were filling forms. Here is what actually happens the moment someone trusts us with their brain."
— Dr. Sweta Adatia, MBBS MD DNB(Neurology) FACP MBA(Cambridge), Founder
1
You arrive. We do not ask for much — just your name, email, and date of birth. Registration
That date of birth is everything. Our system instantly looks at it and routes you to the correct program — Quest for a child between 12 and 14, Evolve for a teenager from 15 to 18, Summit for an adult. No dropdown. No confusion. The brain science knows your stage of life already. Under the hood, the Laravel backend writes your record to the users table in MySQL, hashes your details, and stamps a dob field that drives every decision from here.
2
You pay once. We unlock a lifetime of insight. Stripe Cashier
Stripe handles your card. When payment clears, a webhook fires instantly to our server. Our StripeWebhookController receives it, verifies the signature, and calls EntitlementService to write your package slug — decodemybrain-deep-dive or equivalent — to the users table. The RequirePaidPackage middleware now opens every assessment route for you. This takes less than two seconds after you click Pay.
3
An email arrives with your key. Laravel Mail
Immediately after entitlement is confirmed, our mail system sends your credentials — username, auto-generated password, and a direct link to app.decodemybrain.com. The email goes from info@limitlessbrain.com. There is no waiting room. You open the email, click the link, and you are inside.
4
The assessment begins. 100+ questions that reveal your whole brain. QuestionsController
Your questionnaire is not a personality quiz. It is a neuroscience-backed whole-brain map. The QuestionsController serves questions one at a time from the questions table, storing each answer in question_answers linked to your session. Beyond the core 100+ questions, there are dimensional questions (left-brain / right-brain balance), an introvert–extrovert dimension, a skill assessment, and interactive game-style tasks — all gated by the AuthQuestionAnswered middleware so you cannot skip ahead. Your progress is saved after every single answer, so a refresh never loses work.
5
Your brain profile is computed. Scoring Engine
When you submit the last answer, our scoring engine reads all your responses from question_answers, dimensional_question_answers, introvert_extrovert_question_answers, and skill_test_question_answers. It calculates your scores across five brain dimensions — Analytical, Creative, Emotional, Practical, and Intuitive — and writes them to brain_scores and user_dimensional_score. This is the moment your unique brain blueprint is born. It usually takes under three seconds.
6
Your PDF Report is generated. A personalised blueprint, not a generic printout. BrainResultsController
The BrainResultsController queries your scores and passes them to a Blade PDF template. The template combines your dimension scores, career suggestions, learning strategies, and parenting or self-development insights based on your program. A percentage score per brain quadrant is displayed as a visual chart. The PDF is saved to public/reports_pdfs/ and linked to your dashboard. You can download it at any time. The admin can also access it from the Super Admin panel.
7
You book a coach. We close the loop. Consultation Booking
After your report, your dashboard shows a booking option. Clicking it takes you to a two-step consultation booking flow — pick a time slot, confirm. The booking is saved to the database and an email notification goes to the DecodeMyBrain team. This is optional — the report alone is a complete, stand-alone deliverable. The coaching session deepens it.

05 — Corporate / Bulk Flow
Offline Payment · Batch Codes · Super Admin Visibility
Corporate clients pay offline (invoice / bank transfer). Super Admin generates a unique batch code with a seat limit. The code goes to the corporate contact who shares it with their team. Each employee uses the code to unlock their assessment — no individual payment. Super Admin sees every employee's progress in real time.

No Stripe involved for corporate. Seat count enforced server-side — the (N+1)th use is rejected automatically. Code format to be confirmed with client (length, expiry, alphanumeric pattern).
